# Master profile

*The evidence bank: every bullet on every CV and every sentence in every cover letter traces back to something here. It is written by `/start` from the person's CV and answers, and edited by the person. It lives in `profile/` and is never
published. **Nothing here is invented**: if it is not in the CV or in what the person said, it is not here.*
