# CV and cover-letter conventions: Europe (outside the home market)

Conventions differ country by country. Use the posting's language and the host country's habits; when unsure, a clean two-page reverse-chronological CV
in the posting's language is accepted everywhere.

| Country | CV | Letter | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| France | "CV", one page for students, Madame/Monsieur salutation, "Cordialement" | "Lettre de motivation", one page | A photo is common but optional. |
| Spain, Portugal, Italy | Short CV, often with a photo in Italy and Spain | "Carta de presentacion" / "Carta de apresentacao" / "Lettera di presentazione" | Europass format is accepted by public bodies. |
| Netherlands, Belgium | Two pages, direct tone | "Motivatiebrief" | No photo expected. |
| Ireland, UK | "CV", two pages, no photo, no date of birth | Covering letter, one page | UK/Irish applicants use British spelling. |
| Nordic countries | One to two pages, informal tone | Short, direct | No photo expected in Sweden and Denmark; some employers use their own forms. |
| Switzerland, Austria | As Germany (see regions/de/cv-conventions.md) | As Germany | A photo is more common in Switzerland. |

Document words and salutations per language live in resources/cv_headers.json; add a language there to have the package checks (scripts/consistency_check.py) recognise it.
