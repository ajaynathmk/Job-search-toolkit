# Country notes: Europe

*Researched with web search on 4 October 2026. Thresholds and rules change every year: **verify every figure on the official site before relying on it**,
and refresh a country with `/region` when you start searching it seriously. The "student / trainee work" column assumes a non-EU student at a university
in another EU state.*

| Country | Student / trainee work | Skilled-worker route (figures as researched) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Netherlands** | Erasmus traineeship possible; a work authorisation (TWV) is needed unless the internship is a mandatory curriculum element. EU agencies' traineeships are often EU/EEA nationals only. | Highly skilled migrant: employer must be a **recognised sponsor**; gross monthly €5,942 (age 30+), **€4,357 (under 30)**, €3,122 under the reduced graduate criterion. The **orientation year (zoekjaar)** requires a Master's from a Dutch or top-ranked university; check the current ranking lists. | Pharmacometrics, life-science and tech groups cluster around Leiden, Amsterdam, Utrecht, Eindhoven. |
| **France** | Erasmus traineeship (*stage*) possible. An *alternance* (apprenticeship) is tied to French enrolment and is not usable. | Talent Passport "qualified employee" with a Master's: **€39,582** gross a year minimum; Blue-Card-type highly qualified: **€59,373**. | The salary floor is low enough to be realistic. |
| **Portugal** | Erasmus traineeship possible. | Tech Visa (certified employers; usually several years' experience); Highly Qualified Activity visa; the job-seeker visa needs a Portuguese offer first. | Lower pay market; many multinationals run shared-service centres. |
| **Ireland** (not Schengen) | Own national rules; the Directive is not applied. | **Critical Skills Employment Permit**: graduate threshold **€36,848** (degree completed within 12 months), otherwise **€40,904** (from 1 March 2026), only for occupations on the Critical Skills list. | Strong pay and multinational density in pharma and tech. Check the occupation list. A good post-graduation target. |
| **Belgium** | Erasmus possible. | Not researched. | Pharma R&D at Beerse, Wavre and Brussels. Many internships are open only to PhD or PharmD candidates; ask about Master's places. |
| **Switzerland** (Schengen, not EU) | Own rules; non-EU quota system is tight. | Not researched. | Highest pay in Europe; stretch for a non-EU applicant, needs a permit check before applying. |
| **Denmark** | Own national rules (Directive not applied). | Rules were being eased for some non-EU nationalities (June 2025): check whether yours is on the list. | Large pharma and biotech employers in the Copenhagen area. |
| **Sweden** | Not researched. | Work-permit salary floor planned to rise to SEK 33,390 a month (June 2026). | Strong life-science sector in Gothenburg, Stockholm, Uppsala. |
| **Austria** | Not researched. | Red-White-Red card (points system); check the shortage-occupation list. | German-language market like Germany. |
| **Spain, Italy, Poland, Czechia, Hungary** | Not researched. | Not researched. | Several are shared-service-centre locations for large employers: postings are often English. |

## How to add or refresh a country

1. Search the immigration authority's own page for: student work limits, internship rules (mandatory vs voluntary), post-study permit, skilled-worker permit with salary threshold.
2. Add the finding to the table above with the date and the source URL.
3. If the country needs different scoring than the generic EU rules, add a route rule to profile/region.override.json (rules/regions.md explains the format).
