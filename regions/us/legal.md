# United States: working as an international student or graduate

*Not legal advice. Your school's designated school official (DSO) and an immigration attorney decide each case, and the rules, fees and caps change by year:
read the current rules on the official pages named below before relying on anything here. If you are a U.S. citizen or permanent resident none of this applies,
and the toolkit adds no route adjustment (profile work_authorization.status = citizen or permanent_resident).*

## F-1 students

| Situation | Rule | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Job on campus | F-1 students may work on campus up to 20 hours a week while school is in session, full time during breaks | No separate application; follow your school's rules. |
| Internship during studies | **Curricular Practical Training (CPT)**: training that is an integral part of the degree program, authorized by your DSO and shown on your I-20 before you start | Get authorization before the start date. Twelve months or more of full-time CPT ends eligibility for OPT. |
| After graduation | **Optional Practical Training (OPT)**: up to 12 months of work in your field, applied for through the DSO and USCIS; a **STEM OPT extension** (24 months) is available to graduates of eligible STEM degrees working for an employer enrolled in E-Verify, with a training plan (Form I-983) | Apply in the window before the program end date; a gap with no work can count against the unemployment limit. |
| Long term | **H-1B** or another employer-sponsored status | An employer must file; H-1B has an annual cap and a selection process and its fees and rules change: read uscis.gov for the current ones. |

## What the scoring does with this

- A student on F-1: internships, student jobs and thesis work score as usual (route CPT or on-campus); entry-level roles score as OPT roles; experienced roles lose points because
  they need sponsorship.
- A posting that says it will not sponsor lowers the score for a student (OPT may still cover the first period) and **blocks** it for someone who needs sponsorship from day one.
- "U.S. citizens only" and security-clearance requirements are walls.
