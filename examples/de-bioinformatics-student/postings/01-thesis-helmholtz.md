Master Thesis: Single-cell RNA-seq analysis of immune cell states

About the group
Our group at the Example Helmholtz Institute develops computational methods for single-cell genomics and applies them to immune-mediated diseases.

Your tasks
- Analyse a large single-cell RNA-seq data set (preprocessing, clustering, differential expression) with Python and R
- Build a reproducible pipeline with Snakemake on our Linux HPC cluster
- Compare statistical models for differential expression and write up the results in your thesis

Your profile
- Enrolled in a master's programme in bioinformatics, computational biology, statistics or a related field
- Good programming skills in Python or R, first experience with RNA-seq data
- Working language is English; German is not required

We offer a supervised thesis of six months, a place in a friendly team and access to the cluster. The thesis can start any time from January.
