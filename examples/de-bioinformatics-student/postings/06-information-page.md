Thesis regulations and guidelines

This page lists the formal rules for master's theses at the faculty. Theses must be registered with the examination office. The length of a thesis is described in the examination regulations. Download the forms below. The examination office is open on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Questions about the regulations are answered by the examination board. The faculty publishes the dates of the thesis colloquia each semester, and the library offers workshops on citation and on writing a thesis. Students who need a different deadline can file a request with the board. Please read the guidelines before registering a thesis, and keep the confirmation of registration with your records.
