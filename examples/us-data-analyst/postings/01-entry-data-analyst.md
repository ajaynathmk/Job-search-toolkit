Data Analyst, New Grad

About the role
Join our analytics team and help clinics understand their patients and operations.

What you will do
- Write SQL against our warehouse and build dashboards in Tableau for operations leaders
- Design and analyse A/B tests with the product team
- Present insights and recommendations in weekly reviews

What you bring
- Bachelor's or master's degree in statistics, mathematics, economics or a related field
- Strong SQL and Python skills; experience with Tableau or Power BI
- Clear written and spoken English

We offer competitive pay, health benefits and a hybrid schedule in Austin. Apply online with your resume.
